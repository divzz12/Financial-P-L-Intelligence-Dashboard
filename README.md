# 📈 Financial P&L Intelligence Dashboard

An end-to-end financial analytics solution designed to automate Profit & Loss (P&L) reporting, streamline budget vs. actual variance analysis, and identify operational cost redundancies.

---

## 📌 Business Problem

The finance department was bogged down by a highly manual monthly closing process, spending **3 full days every month** compiling and reconciling budget vs. actual operational expenditure spreadsheets across departments[cite: 3]. This manual dependency slowed down strategic decision-making and increased the risk of reporting errors[cite: 3].

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

* **Power BI**: Interactive financial dashboarding, dynamic drill-downs, and matrix reporting[cite: 3].
* **SQL**: Data pipeline automation, ETL transformation, and staging table development[cite: 3].
* **Microsoft Excel**: Baseline validation, budget mapping, and preliminary financial auditing[cite: 3].

---

## ⚙️ Analysis & Methodology

1. **Automated Data Pipeline**: Designed and deployed an automated SQL database pipeline that extracts, cleans, and aggregates raw ledger data[cite: 3].
2. **Dynamic Dashboard Integration**: Connected the SQL data pipeline directly to Power BI to enable real-time updates and eliminate manual Excel consolidation[cite: 3].
3. **Drill-Down Capability**: Structured dynamic matrix hierarchy levels to allow leadership to seamlessly drill down from high-level P&L line items to individual departmental vendor expenses[cite: 3].

---

## 💡 Key Insights Discovered

* **Cost Inflation Alert**: Uncovered an unexpected **15% inflation in vendor software licensing costs** across non-engineering teams[cite: 3].
* **Subscription Overlap**: Pinpointed redundant tools and unassigned seats within software spending that contributed directly to margin erosion[cite: 3].

---

## 📈 Quantifiable Results & Business Impact

* **Efficiency Boost**: Reduced monthly reporting preparation time from **3 days to just 10 minutes** (a ~99% decrease in prep time)[cite: 3].
* **Direct Cost Savings**: Saved **$14,000 annually** by identifying and eliminating redundant software subscriptions[cite: 3].
